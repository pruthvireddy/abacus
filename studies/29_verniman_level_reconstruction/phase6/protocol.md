# Prospective protocol 6.0.0

This is an estimation/falsification study of the unchanged Phase 5 engineering
artifact. It makes no validated-method acceptance claim, sets no accuracy
threshold for promotion, and selects no replacement configuration. The
[settings](settings.json), [calendar](calendar.json), [input manifest](input-manifest.json)
and [freeze](freeze.json) bind the executable design before observations.

## Claims and fixed mechanism

Observable map agreement concerns independently captured functional divider and
target prices, stated roles and complete ordering. Its formal prospective stratum
requires a timely blinded prediction and a readable, unambiguous, independently
supported pre-09:25 full source revision. Unknown/later revision availability
supports separately labelled captured-revision geometry only. It does not become
pre-open truth from a displayed post timestamp.

Source-method fidelity additionally requires supported family, unadjusted
contract, exact construction/window identity and essential source conditions.
Unknown source windows and VIX/CVD remain unavailable; zero eligible observations
means **not estimable**. Observable price/role agreement cannot satisfy the failed
Phase 5 identifiability gate or establish recovery of Verniman's algorithm.

Retain P0-D1 as the primary engineering configuration and P0-D0, B0-D0, B0-D1,
B1-D0 and B1-D1 as predeclared descriptive comparators. Also retain the immutable
Autopivot primary/alternate pivot comparator separately, with no target meaning
assigned to its alternate. No thresholds, profile windows, branches, target
priorities, anchors, tolerances or missing-family policies change. PBT anchors
remain empty. The target cap remains three per branch, strict outward ordering
and spacing remain, and a fallback pivot abstains from both target branches.

## Capture, actors and source separation

Use the [prediction workflow](daily-capture.md) and separate
[collector instructions](source-collection.md). Record actual actor identities,
roles, answer accesses and disclosures. The prediction operator must not read any
scheduled source answer before the complete-period gate. A collector/scorer may
preserve scheduled source material without conveying it to the prediction
operator. A shared directory or actor label is not access isolation; no operating
system or account enforcement is claimed. Reusing the same actual actor for both
roles cannot establish independence.

The capture tool records its actual process clock, request/native evidence and
hashes. These are local timestamp evidence, not independent certification. A fresh
pre-process access log covers best-known history through no more than five
minutes before start; the final whole-period audit checks the remaining period.
The tool does not import answer loaders. A self-declared time, file modification
time or hash alone cannot certify historical existence or non-access.

Timely means a complete identified map produced prospectively after the confirmed
cutoff and completed by 09:30 on that scheduled date, provisionally subject to
the final actor audit. Late captures, historical Replay reconstructions, exposed
records, unsupported maps and explicit missing/input failures keep distinct
statuses. No future observation or premature missing status may be written.
Retain failed attempts and immutable original receipts. Later exposure findings
change the final ledger's effective classification, never the original receipt.

## Source and revision policy

Collect only exact scheduled-date sources through the existing in-app-browser X
intake rules: normal logged-in UI, no APIs, direct scraping, hidden endpoints,
alternative browsers or messages to outside parties. Retain post/revision/capture
clocks, source class, full images/crop limitations, original labels/intervals,
conditions, divider, full ordered destinations, execution/structural annotations,
contract/window evidence, uncertainty and conflicts. Null availability stays null.

The canonical formal plan is the earliest independently captured, full,
unambiguous pre-cutoff plan revision for that date. Evidence must bind the same
revision/full artifact and actual collector access before cutoff; a displayed post
time alone is insufficient. Unresolved tied/conflicting plans remain separate
strata and do not supply a unique primary divider. Later revisions, closing
annotations and recollections remain descriptive source classes. Never choose a
revision by agreement with predictions or use an execution arrow to supply a
missing pre-open target. Identical opposite branch triggers form one functional
divider only under the unchanged exact-interval convention.

## Complete-period reveal and incomplete policy

No answers are released to the prediction operator until **after December 8,
16:00 New York (21:00 UTC)** and a successful machine gate. Every one of the
20 dates must have exactly one explicit terminal status, a retained immutable
capture/status receipt, valid input/producer identities where applicable, complete
actor chronology and final ledger. A fresh best-known final access audit must
close within five minutes before gate creation and after the last scheduled
close; it cannot claim future non-access. The gate reads metadata only; the
scorer's later audit must preserve the history and account for subsequent access.
A failure receipt cannot be a full map.
Missing data never deletes a date or extends the sample.

The gate may authorize post-period descriptive scoring of a fully accounted
incomplete/exposed batch. It must retain the contamination flags and forbid
formal prospective estimation for any batch with early prediction-operator
answer access/disclosure. Absence of known exposure is an audited attestation,
not independently enforced blinding. A pending/incompletely accounted batch,
changed prediction or invalid identity stays closed. Scoring consumes saved
predictions only; selection is never rerun after reveal.

## Metrics, denominators and uncertainty

Report all 20 scheduled outcomes and capture/source yields first. For formal
observable aggregate estimation require at least **15 distinct dates** with a
timely blinded complete map and unambiguous authenticated source divider, and
at least **15 distinct dates for each target branch**. An aggregate end-to-end
map endpoint additionally needs 15 common dates eligible for all three roles.
These are administrative
coverage minima, not power calculations, accuracy acceptance criteria or a
method-validation threshold. Below a minimum, that endpoint is insufficient for
the preregistered aggregate; retain per-session/descriptive results. Actual
denominators are printed for every endpoint. Do not infer success from missingness.

Carry the global one-point maximum-cardinality, minimum-interval-gap,
lexicographic one-to-one contract within source class/revision/branch strata.
Preserve feasible alternatives, equal optima, compound roles counted once and
all source destinations beyond the executable cap. Actual point/E1 boundary
error is reported separately from uncertainty-interval gap. Interval overlap
alone cannot establish exact boundary reproduction.

Report candidate availability, supported construction/window identity, pivot
price/functional-role agreement, selected target coverage/extras, complete ordered
branch reproduction, conditional-on-candidate-availability selection and
end-to-end map agreement. Retain fallback/abstention, partial ladders, unsupported
families and missing sources. An abstention remains in the end-to-end source
denominator; undefined ordering is null, not a fabricated observed false order.
A matched subsequence is not a complete ladder. Construction-qualified metrics
remain separate from interval-qualified geometry throughout.

Report actual-error MAE, median, quantiles/range and per-session paired comparator
differences on common units. For date-level binary outcomes, nominal 95% Wilson
intervals may describe sampling uncertainty under an independent-Bernoulli
working assumption. Overlapping histories, serial dependence, selected sources
and at most 20 dates mean those intervals are not calibrated generalization
guarantees. No threshold, multiple-comparison winner, configuration switch,
profitability inference or validated-method declaration is permitted.
