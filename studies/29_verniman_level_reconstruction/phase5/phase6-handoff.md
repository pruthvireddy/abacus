## Proposed prospective eligibility route

A later authorization should establish an unseen period after the final freeze.
The following calendar proposal is explicit but **not adopted or executed here**:
use the first **20 exchange-calendar full RTH sessions** after that authorization
and freeze, with the calendar, start rule, contract and scheduled dates fixed
before any prediction or source access. No exact replacement dates or answers
were inspected to propose this rule. Confirm the instrument remains suitable
for the whole block before starting; a contract/input-policy change needs a new
declared identity rather than a silent rollover.

Every scheduled date stays in the report. A missing input, shortened session,
source absence or prediction failure retains its status; do not extend the
sample to replace a bad result. Any calendar exclusions must be declared from
independent calendar information before evaluation, not inferred from answers.
If the proposed block cannot meet the unchanged implementation's declared
requirements, the plan remains ineligible until a separately authorized protocol
resolves the design before predictions.

1. **Exposure audit and operator separation.** Audit known study/team exposure
   metadata without copying prices. Use a prediction operator without source
   access and an independent scorer. Constrain both to bounded, reviewed guidance;
   do not read mutable general guides, galleries or unrestricted source tables.
   Distinguish known non-exposure from an unverifiable claim about all account
   activity. Independence helps enforce future blinding but does not repair the
   original study history.
2. **Final pre-prediction freeze.** Pin one configuration or an explicitly bounded
   comparison family, all implementation/dependency bytes, settings, contract,
   feed, calendar, sample, source/revision rules, metrics, common denominators,
   tolerances, missing-data treatment and numerical acceptance thresholds. Preserve
   the development outcome even if it is no-selection. Any substantive new rule
   is a proposed future version, not an unseen success of the existing artifact.
3. **Prospective prediction capture.** For each scheduled date, retain the exact
   completed 09:20–09:25 bar, identified preceding history, complete candidate and
   rejection ledgers, selected IDs/original coordinates/reasons, fallback and
   abstention status. Record a trustworthy capture time and immutable hashes
   before the operator sees any answer. Keep post-cutoff prices/acceptance unable
   to revise the saved map. Missing causal anchors stay missing.
4. **Separate source capture.** A separately scoped collector may preserve dated
   source revisions and timestamps without conveying answer content to the
   prediction operator. Record historical-availability qualifications honestly;
   a later capture timestamp does not establish earlier availability. Do not
   repair null availability or infer exact source construction from a nearby price.
5. **Complete-batch reveal gate.** Before answer review, machine-check all scheduled
   prediction records, producer/input/settings identities, native scope and
   chronology. A failure placeholder or baseline-only map cannot masquerade as
   a complete expanded prediction. Apply the predeclared incomplete-batch policy;
   never manufacture a pre-reveal record after a source has been read.
6. **Independent fixed scoring.** After the declared gate passes, use exact-date
   intake only. Preserve every source revision/class/branch, conflicting trigger,
   interval, compound label and destination beyond the three-target cap. Score
   saved predictions without rerunning selection or changing eligibility.

## Proposed metrics and acceptance gates

Report engineering readiness separately from source-method evidence. The future
protocol should carry the existing one-point maximum-cardinality/minimum-gap/
lexicographic one-to-one contract, exact family/contract/window compatibility,
actual point/E1-boundary error separately from interval gap, source strata,
candidate availability, conditional selection, complete branch ordering, end-to-end
agreement, extras, abstention/fallbacks and partial ladders. Report every common
eligible denominator and every scheduled missing/conflicting case. A matched
subsequence is not a complete ladder; uncertainty overlap is not exact boundary
agreement.

Engineering acceptance requires all required input/identity and causal cutoff
gates, the declared native verification scope, and successful saved-prediction
integrity checks. It does not establish source fidelity. A source-method
comparison additionally requires independently supported source windows and
constructions, sufficient predeclared common units, and the required source
conditions. VIX/CVD cannot be silently replaced with another feature. If these
requirements remain absent, source-method performance stays **not estimable**,
even on genuinely unseen dates.

The Phase 5 decision rule requires at least three of four development dates'
common eligible pivot/branch units and a unique alternative that is no worse on
all declared agreement/extras measures, strictly better on at least one and does
not increase abstention. That bounded development rule is not an automatically
powered 20-session confirmatory test. A future authorization must predeclare its
own required common denominator, quantitative acceptance thresholds and uncertainty
reporting **before** prospective predictions. This design adopts no post-result
threshold and claims no recovery of a proprietary method or trading profitability.

## Final readiness statement after development

All four complete development predictions and all four native primary
cutoff/advance/restart records passed their gate before the narrow answer intake.
No validated alternative was selected. P0-D1 was retained only as the declared
engineering default. Common eligible primary dates are **0/4** for pivot and
both target branches, against the frozen requirement of at least three per role;
strict source-method agreement, conditional strict selection and strict
end-to-end reproduction remain **not estimable**, not zero-percent success.
VIX/CVD conditions and historical source availability remain unresolved.

The descriptive P0 pivot error ties B1 and is smaller than B0, while some D1
coordinate pairs improve on D0. No complete canonical coordinate ladder was
recovered. Those geometry results cannot satisfy the strict identifiability or
dominance gate and did not switch the artifact. Four dates do not establish
generalization or recovery of a proprietary method.

No genuinely unseen final period has been established, adopted or authorized.
The original exposed period remains qualified even for a new evaluator. Until
separate authorization, a prospective sample, exposure controls, source-identity
eligibility and a full future protocol exist, **Phase 6 is not ready to execute**.
The fixed engineering artifact is retained for a separately scoped future test;
that is distinct from a source-method acceptance claim.

Keep the final freeze manifest beside the development report and link the exact
stage evidence. Preserve all failed and inconclusive results. Do not publish to
Narada, deploy, calculate P&L or run the final hidden test under this handoff.
