primary pairs require readable finite intervals, compatible contract/coordinates,
family and independently supported exact source-window identity. Use global
**one ES point** interval-gap feasibility. Report actual point/E1-boundary error
separately from uncertainty-interval gap. Match one-to-one within date, source
class/revision/branch/family/window by maximum cardinality, minimum total gap,
then lexicographic IDs; retain all feasible/equal-optimum alternatives.

Report input/native readiness, candidate availability, supported construction
identity, pivot placement/role, target sets/extras/order, conditional selection,
end-to-end agreement and fallback/abstention/partial ladders separately. Unknown
windows stay outside primary eligible denominators; no eligible observations
means **not estimable**, not zero percent. Unsupported families and absent causal

units across configurations and every missing/conflicting case. A matched
subsequence is not a complete ordered ladder, and E1 overlap is not exact
boundary agreement. Four development dates cannot establish generalization.
Apply only the separately frozen decision rule; do not force a winner.
