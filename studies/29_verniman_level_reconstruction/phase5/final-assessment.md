## What the descriptive baseline comparison shows

There are four common functional-divider price diagnostics, one per scheduled
date rather than two raw trigger observations per date. P0 and B1 choose the same
pivot price on all four. Their descriptive mean absolute error is **11 points**,
versus **24 points** for B0. Neither P0/B1 nor B0 is within one point on any date,
whether using actual point/boundary error or interval gap. Thus P0 shows no pivot
placement advantage over the nearest-value B1 baseline in this sample. Its
smaller error than fixed-POC B0 remains coordinate evidence, with unresolved
construction/window identity.

For P0, D1 has **3/10 bullish** and **3/9 bearish** coordinate pairs, versus
**3/10** and **1/9** for D0. D1's coordinate extras are six bullish/four bearish;
D0's are six/eight. B0-D1 and B1-D1 have the same paired source counts as P0-D1,
with different selection counts and abstention behavior. These are interval-
qualified geometry diagnostics, not construction-qualified target coverage.
They do not supply the strict Pareto vector required to change the default.

No complete canonical coordinate ladder was recovered. P0's October 16 fallback
abstains from both branches; its ordering results remain unassessed/null rather
than being fabricated as an observed false order. All four that day's source
destinations remain in the source denominators. D1's October 15/19 bearish
ladders remain partial. The full 19 canonical destinations and all 98 retained
source occurrences remain available in the source/report artifacts; structural,
recollected and later annotations are not pooled into canonical target totals.

All source historical availability fields remain null. The October 20 canonical
revision also reports a claimed update after the 09:25 cutoff; it cannot be
treated as certified pre-open truth. Closing labels and execution arrows do not
repair timing or missing scenario evidence. The four dates do not establish
generalization, and neither a narrower error nor an uncertainty overlap recovers
Verniman's construction or full conditions.
