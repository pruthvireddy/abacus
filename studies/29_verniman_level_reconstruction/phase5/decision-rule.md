# Pre-answer development decision rule

This rule is frozen before development prediction execution or answer access.
It evaluates exactly the six configurations listed in settings.json. It never
requires a winner or authorizes changing candidate/selector mathematics.

1. **Engineering gate.** All four complete expanded maps, six-policy outputs,
   identified input/producer hashes, and native P0-D1 cutoff/advance/restart
   records must pass the reveal gate. Otherwise answers remain closed, the
   development comparison is incomplete and final evaluation is not ready.
2. **Identifiability gate.** A supported source-method configuration comparison
   requires common eligible source-class/revision units on at least three of the
   four scheduled dates for the pivot and both ordered branches. Primary units
   require supported family/contract/exact-window identity. Any essential
   unmeasured source condition remains separately unavailable. Missing/conflicting
   plans are not repaired and revisions are not chosen by proximity. A zero or
   insufficient common eligible denominator makes the mechanism comparison
   inconclusive/not estimable. Coordinate errors cannot pass this gate.
3. **Predeclared dominance rule when identifiable.** Compare common units using
   pivot identity/role-and-price agreement, complete ordered branch agreement,
   end-to-end eligible-source recall, and extra selections per branch. A declared
   alternative can replace P0-D1 only if it is no worse on every one of these
   measures, strictly better on at least one, and does not increase abstention.
   It must be the unique dominating configuration; multiple conflicting or tied
   alternatives produce no change. Conditional-on-candidate-availability results
   explain failures separately, without removing first-pass misses from the
   end-to-end denominator. Do not turn overlapping confidence/uncertainty into
   independent confirmations or choose a metric after reveal.
4. **No forced promotion.** If the engineering gate passes but identifiability or
   unique dominance fails, retain P0-D1 only as the previously declared engineering
   default and record **no validated configuration selected**. Report every
   baseline and descriptive coordinate result, including unfavorable outcomes.
   Even if strict dominance exists, four dates support only this bounded
   development comparison, not reliable generalization or recovered methodology.
5. **Final-evaluation status.** Separately assess whether a genuinely unseen final
   evaluation can be established. The original hidden period is compromised;
   a fresh evaluator cannot erase that study history. Do not execute Phase 6 or
   inspect replacement answers. A future prospective period requires separate
   authorization and prediction/source-timing freezes before any answers. If
   exact source identities or an unexposed evaluation are unavailable, state
   that source-method final evaluation is not ready, while preserving the
   frozen engineering artifact for a separately scoped future test.

Descriptive pivot MAE, interval-gap overlap, target-set geometry and relative
order are reported with their actual common denominators. They can motivate a
future version, but cannot switch the default under an unidentifiable mechanism
comparison. No substantive improvement is implemented or rescored in this phase.
