5. SETUP FAMILY AND EVENT SEQUENCE

Mandatory ingredients:
- A return toward the daily pivot.
- Interaction with the EMA cloud.
- A completed directional reaction bar.

EMA ordering and slope are optional filters. Cloud interaction remains required even when both filters are OFF.

Use causal state transitions, not an exact reproduction of the example screenshot.

At 09:30, reset setup state. The last completed pre-open bar may establish approach direction, but all qualifying pivot/cloud interactions must occur at or after 09:30.

Define proximity tolerance at the first pivot-return touch:

delta = max(1 ES point, 0.10 × prior completed bar’s ATR14)

Round upward to the ES tick and freeze it for that episode.

A touch means the completed bar’s range intersects the tolerance-expanded pivot or cloud zone. Use completed-bar cloud values, without claiming to know the EMA’s intrabar path.

SHORT — PIVOT/CLOUD REJECTION:
- A previously completed close establishes price below the pivot’s lower edge by at least delta.
- Price subsequently returns to the tolerance-expanded pivot.
- Allow that return bar and the next two bars for the reaction to form.
- During this episode, price must touch both the pivot and cloud. The touches may occur on different bars.
- Require pivot/cloud separation no greater than delta on at least one interaction bar.
- The first bearish bar closing below the cloud’s lower boundary completes the short candidate.

LONG — PIVOT/CLOUD SUPPORT:
- Mirror the short rules.
- Establish approach from above the pivot.
- Require a return toward pivot/cloud.
- The first bullish bar closing above the cloud’s upper boundary completes the long candidate.

Define bearish as close < open and bullish as close > open.

Do not require a giant candle, two consecutive reaction candles, a particular wick ratio, or an EMA crossover. Record such characteristics as descriptive tags rather than adding them to the baseline.

Expire an incomplete return episode after its three-bar formation window.

Document exact boundary comparisons and episode deduplication. Candidate formation must not depend on later trade outcomes.

6. EARLY VERSUS CONFIRMED ENTRIES

Create a common candidate ID at the reaction close, before applying either entry policy.

EARLY:
Enter at the next 5-minute open after the completed reaction bar.

CONFIRMED:
After the same reaction, wait for a completed close beyond BOTH the pivot zone and cloud in the trade direction:
- Short: below pivot lower edge and cloud lower edge.
- Long: above pivot upper edge and cloud upper edge.

Enter at the following 5-minute open.

Confirmation may occur on the reaction bar itself. In that case the two policies coincide. Report this frequency; do not assume “early” always produces an earlier fill.

Expire confirmation after three subsequent bars, structural invalidation, or the entry cutoff, whichever comes first.

Entry fills must satisfy:
09:30 <= fill time < 10:00 New York.

With completed cash-bar reactions, the earliest possible entry is 09:35. A candle closing at 10:00 cannot generate an eligible entry.

Do not mix reaction-low/high stop entries into this comparison. That would be a separately named future model.

7. OPTIONAL EMA FILTERS

Test exactly four masks:
A. No EMA trend filter.
B. EMA ordering only.
C. EMA slope only.
D. Both ordering and slope.

Ordering:
- Long: EMA20 > EMA50.
- Short: EMA20 < EMA50.

Slope:
- Long: both EMAs are above their values three completed bars earlier.
- Short: both EMAs are below their values three completed bars earlier.

Evaluate and freeze these filters at the common reaction close so both entry policies use the same candidate population.

Do not add an entry-time recheck to the primary experiment.

This produces eight entry/filter combinations.

8. STOPS AND TARGETS

Freeze the structural stop when the reaction candidate completes.

Short stop:
max(highest high from first return touch through reaction,
    pivot upper edge) + 1 ES point.

Long stop:
min(lowest low over that episode,
    pivot lower edge) - 1 ES point.

Use the same frozen stop for early and confirmed entries. Do not widen it while confirmation develops.

Cancel an unfilled candidate if its structural stop is reached. Reject an entry whose opening price is already beyond the stop; record the reason.

PRIMARY EXIT:
- Fixed 2R target.
- Structural stop.
- Flatten any remaining position at 11:00 New York.

Define initial R from actual entry fill to the frozen stop. Report net R after costs using that initial risk.

SECONDARY EXIT:
Use the nearest profitable target among the first two saved Verne targets for that direction.
- Use the near edge of a target zone.
- Select using information available at entry.
- Record original target ordinal, description and price.
- If neither target is strictly beyond entry, mark this policy unavailable.
- Do not fall back silently to 2R.

Compare target policies on their common eligible population and separately show their coverage differences.

Do not choose exits trade by trade according to which would have worked.

The full declared comparison is:
2 entry policies × 4 EMA masks × 2 exit policies = 16 variants.

Primary comparison:
Early versus confirmed, EMA filters OFF, fixed 2R exit.

9. EXECUTION AND COSTS

Use one ES contract, no pyramiding, no scaling, no breakeven move and no trailing stop.

Primary portfolio rule:
Use the first qualifying reaction candidate each day, selected before optional EMA filters. A filtered-out candidate does not get replaced by a later candidate.

Allow at most one trade per variant per day.

Use 1-minute sequencing after entry to resolve stops, targets and time exits. Never count target touches that occurred before entry.

If stop and target remain touched within the same finest available bar:
- Use adverse-first ordering for primary results.
- Report the ambiguity count and optimistic bound.
- Do not silently infer a favorable price path.

Model gaps through stops at the worse available opening price, plus applicable slippage.

- $5 round-trip fees per contract, explicitly labelled an assumption.
- One tick adverse slippage per market/stop execution.
- Conservative target-limit fills requiring one tick of trade-through.
