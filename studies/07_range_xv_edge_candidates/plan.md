# Range XV edge candidates

Study `07_range_xv_edge_candidates` tests three fixed, previously motivated hypotheses on ES 6RXV, NQ 20RXV, and GC 20RXV. The observation unit is a completed Range XV bar in an inferred 18:00-based session. ATAS describes Range XV as suppressing quiet-market bars and highlighting counteraction; its construction algorithm is undisclosed. The historical alignment and candle-quality studies found short-horizon conditional effects but no demonstrated profitable rule. In particular, their high one-bar continuation rates coexisted with negative average next-bar chart-price returns. This study tests price payoffs and tail losses, not just hit rates.

Sources: [ATAS Range XV](https://atas.net/blog/specific-of-the-range-xv-chart/), [ATAS chart types](https://help.atas.net/en/support/solutions/articles/72000602350-chart-periods-and-types), [alignment evidence](../../runs/03_multi_rxv_alignment/20260908T_multi_rxv_v1/summary.md), and [quality evidence](../../runs/04_reversal_candle_quality/20260908T_reversal_quality_v1/summary.md).

## Frozen hypotheses

1. **One-bar pullback resumption.** At least three completed bars in one direction, exactly one opposite bar, then a completed return bar in the original direction whose close passes the opposing bar's high/low. The primary outcome is the signed next-open to following-open return, compared with all unfiltered snapbacks after the same prior run. Snapbacks without the extreme break and ordinary directional bars after a three-bar run are descriptive context. Body at least 70% and adverse wick below 20% of range are declared subgroups, not an optimized rule.
2. **Extended-run exhaustion.** After at least eight completed same-direction bars, compare a trade after the first opposite bar with a policy that trades only after a second consecutive opposite bar. Every early opportunity remains in the policy denominator; nonconfirmation means no wait-policy trade and zero policy return. The primary outcome is signed three-future-bar open-to-open return per opportunity, compared with first reversals after runs of one or two bars. One- and five-bar outcomes are secondary.
3. **Quiet-period breakout.** A first burst bar follows a low preceding 30-minute bar-start count, the next bar shares its direction and closes beyond the preceding ten completed bars' extreme. Require a complete 30-minute lookback within the inferred session for both calibration and events. Low activity is the bottom quintile of the development-period count distribution for the same chart and export-clock half-hour. The primary outcome is signed three-future-bar open-to-open return against the same two-bar breakout without the low-activity condition.

## Timing, sample, and comparisons

Use only the seven pinned Range XV files; the three named charts are primary and the other exports remain provenance/context. Source timestamps are interpreted as bar starts. A bar is observable only at its next bar's start within the same inferred session. Require `next_bar_start_proxy`, so equal-second bars and session-final bars are excluded from primary signals. An immediate chart-price reference enters at the next bar's open; a one-bar-delay sensitivity enters one bar later and holds the same number of future bars. Exits use a subsequent bar's open, after the planned count of completed future bars. Never cross an inferred session boundary. Exclude the partial 2026-09-08 session. An ordinary long gap is not automatically a data defect.

Use full observed sessions as primary. The 09:30–10:30 export-clock subgroup is secondary and is **not** labeled New York time. Analyze assets independently; use matched dates for cross-asset display. Freeze development 2024-08-14–2025-11-10, validation 2025-11-11–2026-04-10, and final block 2026-04-13–2026-09-07. Prior studies inspected all of this history, so even the final block is retrospective, not pristine out-of-sample evidence.

Suppress overlapping trades separately for each policy and asset by accepting the earliest eligible opportunity, then the next whose entry follows the previous exit. Show raw and accepted counts. Report one-, three-, and five-bar chart-price returns where declared, means/medians, loss rate, fifth-percentile return, worst-session concentration, break-even round-trip cost, and 1/2/4-tick cost haircuts. Resample inferred sessions for confidence intervals. The nine primary idea-by-asset contrasts use a familywise 99.44% Bonferroni interval. Controls are same-chart, same-direction, same chronological block; their differing opportunity populations are disclosed.

A provisional candidate requires positive primary candidate return after a two-tick haircut and a positive candidate-minus-baseline contrast after the same per-trade cost in both validation and final blocks, plus a positive result after removing the top 1% profit-contributing sessions. A nonpositive familywise lower interval on the net contrast prevents a confirmatory claim. Neither criterion certifies executable profitability: the exports omit exact completion times, bid/ask, intrabar ordering, timezone, and verified contract/adjustment provenance. Any stop/target diagnostic assigns the stop first when both barriers occur in a bar and also reports the optimistic bound. Fresh unseen data and fill-level evidence are necessary to establish a sustainable tradable edge.
