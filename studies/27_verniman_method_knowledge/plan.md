## Execution plan and acceptance criteria

### 1. Locate and preserve the source map

Read Abacus ownership/lifecycle guidance. Resolve the actual menu destinations,
including country-domain aliases and historical slugs; do not guess from titles.
Capture each selected page, access time, resolved URL, SHA-256 and the page's
article body, embedded videos, images and authored outbound links. Treat URL
dates as locators unless an explicit displayed publication date is available.
Keep provider/company lists and schedules as historical source statements.

### 2. Read the material in parallel, with one evidence standard

Review all selected article bodies. Distinguish Vern's own first-person rules,
material selected/reposted from other authors, external explanations, and Abacus
inferences. Record exact supporting page links and unresolved details. Identify
cross-topic dependencies and apparent tensions before writing a synthesis.

### 3. Investigate linked media

Inventory every embedded and directly linked YouTube video in selected article
bodies; deduplicate by video ID while retaining every parent-topic relation.
Open those videos in the in-app browser and use its transcript exporter. Record
title/channel/language when observable, actual transcript retention/hash and
review coverage. Read accessible transcripts, with timestamp locators where
available. A video title, thumbnail, search snippet or page wrapper is not a
transcript. If captions are missing, the video is removed or retrieval fails,
record the exact limitation and continue other sources.

Inspect source charts/diagrams at readable resolution when accessible. Record
what is visible, the source's annotations, our reading, unresolved scales/timing,
and what a static image cannot establish. Prefer a small set of instructive case
studies over invented precision. Do not call an unviewed image reviewed or infer
historical order-book changes from a single frame.

### 4. Write the knowledge base

Provide a navigable entry point, one note per requested topic, a shared glossary,
a connected model of the method, a level-selection framework, practical reading
and execution checklists, exercises, chart cases, and an explicit unknowns list.
Keep source statements separate from synthesized workflow. Preserve conditions,
invalidation, target logic, timing, and missing operational definitions.
Use original explanatory prose; brief quotations only when indispensable. Do not
publish full blog text, transcripts or copied charts as the knowledge base.

### 5. Audit and preserve

Audit 27/27 topic coverage, source and media inventories, attribution, links,
quotations, transcript claims, chart claims and source/inference labels. Check
local documentation links, navigation metadata and generated index freshness.
No numerical suite applies. Integrity/coverage checks do not validate trading.

Stage in `work/27_verniman_method_knowledge/20261023T174000Z_review27/`.
Keep source captures and transcript exports there with hashes and retention
instructions; include portable capture inventories in the completed evidence.
Prepare all authored review content, provenance, settings, input identities,
limitations and checks before sealing a unique run at
`runs/27_verniman_method_knowledge/20261023T174000Z_review27/`.
Completed content stays immutable; later changed interpretation needs a new run.

## Coverage states

Use distinct states for article read, video inventoried, transcript retrieved,
transcript reviewed, visual inspected, inaccessible and not reviewed. A complete
topic note may still have a media gap, which must be prominent. Never declare
the entire method fully recovered while inaccessible sources remain.

## Future empirical work

Any subsequent test must separately freeze causal definitions of value,
acceptance/rejection, time horizon, level availability, entry, stop, target,
costs and exclusions, with pinned inputs. This knowledge base supplies research
questions; it does not supply tested profitability or missing execution rules.
