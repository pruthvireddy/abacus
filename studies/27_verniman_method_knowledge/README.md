## Method review and preservation

The review connects auction context, profile/value structure, level roles,
order-flow observations, execution, risk, practice and psychology. It preserves
the difference between Vern's first-person rules, educational material he
curates from other authors, and Abacus's synthesis. The blog's historical URL
slugs do not establish when its current contents were last edited.

This is qualitative source research, with no Abacus market-data calculation,
backtest, strategy implementation or profitability validation. Reported
percentages and historical trading examples remain attributed source claims.
The current published Verniman daily-level datasets are separate inputs and
are neither modified nor empirically assessed here.

The [settings](study.yaml), [source input declaration](inputs.lock.json),
[topic map](topics.json) and [decisions](notes.md) record scope and provenance.
Captured HTML, image bytes and browser-exported transcripts are retained under
the ignored stage at `work/27_verniman_method_knowledge/20261023T174000Z_review27/`.
The completed review records their paths and SHA-256 hashes. Preserve that
stage as retained source evidence; do not treat its `source-captures/` or
`transcripts/` directories as disposable cache. Full third-party source text
and transcripts are not included in the authored Markdown knowledge base.

The completed run is immutable. Changed interpretations require a new run;
update this study's selected reference only after reviewing the replacement.
