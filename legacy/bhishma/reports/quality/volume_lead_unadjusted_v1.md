## Methodology and session convention

Databento `v.0` mappings select the active outright contract. `volume_lead_v1` applies a mapping dated `D` at 18:00 America/New_York on the preceding calendar day, the open of the Globex session labeled `D`. The normal close is 17:00 ET and 17:00–18:00 ET is treated as the maintenance break. DST is timezone-aware. Mapping `d1` is exclusive.

OHLC prices remain Databento signed int64 fixed-point values at scale `1e-9`; volume and prices are copied unchanged. No rows are aggregated, filled, interpolated, or adjusted.

## Known limitations

- The session calendar uses the documented recurring Globex boundary rule; it does not invent holiday or early-close bars.
- Volume-ranked rolls are Databento mappings and are not claimed to reproduce TradingView exactly.
- Prices are real selected-contract prices and therefore jump at contract rolls.
- Degraded source dates remain present and must be considered by downstream research.
