# Dated interpretation — 2026-09-24

After a first countertrend C1 following at least three prior trend bars, C2
confirms roughly three quarters of eligible C1s. At the C2 decision, both C3
and C4 follow in about 40–43% of confirmed cases (see `tables/overall.csv`).
Compared with ordinary same-direction B1/B2 pairs at the same chart, direction,
and decision-time window, eight of 70 historical contrasts pass the frozen
familywise and chronological screen (`tables/headline_screen.csv`). The positive
cells are concentrated in GC and bearish NQ 10RXV, and are only about 1–2
percentage points above their ordinary baselines. Matching ordinary pairs to
run age two was added after seeing the primary result. Seven selected
contrasts narrowed and one widened; all eight remained positive. These
post-result contrasts are exploratory (`tables/age2_comparison.csv`).

The decision to wait must be read on the original C1 population. Requiring
confirmation and then two more bars succeeds on about 30–33% of eligible C1s,
compared with about 47–50% for immediate C2+C3 continuation; these count
different two-bar horizons (`tables/opportunity_overall.csv`). The signed
open-to-open chart-price proxy for waiting averages below zero for every chart
and direction, and is worse than the immediate proxy in 11 of 14 pairs
(`tables/price.csv`, `tables/price_delta.csv`). These are unadjusted chart
references, not fills or strategy returns. The high one-bar snapback C2 rate
does not transfer to a two-more rate (`tables/snapback.csv`).

Do not promote a selected cell to a trading rule from this sample. The
historical periods overlap prior RXV inspection; chart settings share trades;
the normalized clock and selected GC sessions carry documented uncertainty.
The next gate is a genuinely unseen later export with ATAS timezone, session,
feed, and contract settings verified, followed by an execution and cost study
only if a preregistered chart-state difference persists.
