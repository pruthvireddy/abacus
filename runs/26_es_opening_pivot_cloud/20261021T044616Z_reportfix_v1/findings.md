# Findings — protocol 1.0.0

Run `20261021T044616Z_reportfix_v1` retains 754 eligible sessions from 2022-05-16 through 2026-09-18. Both long support and short rejection were tested. The first daily candidate was fixed before filters; 98 total candidates were retained and 85 first candidates entered the primary early policy.

The primary model is unsupported as a profitable entry strategy in this sample. In `tables/variant_summary.csv`, mask A/fixed 2R early produced 85 trades, −$15,425 net and −$20.45756 per input-eligible session; confirmed produced 82 trades, −$12,485 net and −$16.55836 per eligible session. All eight fixed-2R entry/filter variants had negative net results after the reference fee/slippage assumptions.

Early superiority is unsupported. `tables/primary_comparison.csv`, pooled_exploratory, gives early minus confirmed −$3.89920/session, 95% day-cluster bootstrap [−$9.98674, +$0.04973]. The temporal_test row has 149 eligible sessions and 16 both-filled trades: early −$33.03691/session versus confirmed −$27.04698, difference −$5.98993 with interval [−$17.96980,$0]. The difference remains inconclusive, and the small temporal-test trade count is weak evidence. Of 82 both-filled primary candidates, 81 coincided; only one pair had a different entry time. Three were early-only, none confirmed-only, 669 neither.

EMA ordering/both filters reduce pooled loss and participation, but establish no profitable fixed-2R variant. Early B/C/D retained 68/65/53 trades versus 85; confirmed 66/63/52 versus 82. B and D improved pooled per-trade as well as per-session point estimates; slope alone worsened R expectancy in both policies and worsened early dollar expectancy; confirmed dollar expectancy modestly improved from −$152.26 to −$147.46 per trade. See `tables/variant_summary.csv`, `tables/secondary_comparisons.csv` for the declared Holm family and `tables/splits.csv` for temporal evidence. Do not mistake avoiding losing trades for proof of a positive edge.

The saved-target policy has one unavailable session for each entry/mask combination; full input-eligible expectancy remains undefined rather than assigning zero. Every available-cohort saved-target point estimate is negative. `tables/target_comparison.csv` separately identifies common-defined and both-filled comparisons. `tables/robustness.csv` retains negative primary point estimates in all six one-factor sensitivities. Prespecified cuts are descriptive, overlapping and often sparse; they do not establish a subgroup strategy.

Source availability is conditional and retrospective: all selected Verne available_at values remain null and the verified pre-open cohort is empty. No native TradingView parity has been established. The inspiration date 2026-09-15 has no date-specific exclusion, but independently fails ordinary contract matching (ESZ26 versus ESU26) and 250-bar readiness. Input exclusions are retrospectively assessed; no alternative contract, pivot system or readiness policy was substituted.
