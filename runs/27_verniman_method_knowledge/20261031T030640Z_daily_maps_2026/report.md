This local source archive covers every calendar date from January 1 through
October 30, 2026, newest first. It retains 201 dated Daily Maps across 198 days,
two undated commentaries and 417 article images. Dated maps contain 402 images;
the undated commentaries contain 15. All captured article body text is retained,
including plans, notes, schedules, closing boilerplate and outbound links.

The 303 daily records include 19 weekday and 86 weekend gaps. These mean no
observed dated post, not proof that no post ever existed. Three duplicate date
pairs (October 7, September 10 and July 22) remain distinct. No adjacent plan is
substituted into a gap. The two undated March commentaries remain separate.

## Source traversal and date identity

The supplied October 30 post anchored a fresh Older Post traversal. The official
sitemap index and all three declared sitemap pages were captured; every one of
the 202 sitemap locators between the seed and the lower boundary is represented.

Historical permalink years, update metadata and image filenames are not used to
silently replace the source date. Capture time does not establish historical
availability. This archive does not change any released daily-level dataset.

capture. The other 202 article records were freshly fetched. Images with matching

## Storage and verification

The standalone reader is 27,335,747 bytes (26.1 MiB), including source capsules,
images and navigation. Source HTML compresses from 10,182,598 to 2,304,120 bytes.
Original compressed image bytes are preserved without resizing or lossy changes.
The reader has no remote scripts, fonts or image dependencies. Original outbound
links remain references, and embedded media are not claimed as downloaded video.

Full source bytes stay in the retained ignored work stage. This completed run
stores portable date navigation, capture and payload inventories, counts, coverage,
code/environment snapshots and checks. Restore the retained stage to use source
links from a source-only clone. Do not clean it as disposable cache.

1,626 Markdown links. An independent capture audit verified 1,039 unique input,
source and image files, all 203 body date identities, all prior source identities,
all sitemap locators and the lower boundary. Independent text comparison found
complete article character content preserved after whitespace normalization.
Browser inspection verified the latest and earliest articles, full image loading,
duplicate-date display and both qualified 400-pixel images.

These checks verify source retention and presentation. No trading calculations,
