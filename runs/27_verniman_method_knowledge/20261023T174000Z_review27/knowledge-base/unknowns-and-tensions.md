## Statements that require qualification, not harmonization

- **The statistics are not independently supported here.** T06 lacks samples,
  periods, code, denominators and uncertainty. Two 78% statements concern
  different events. Touching one prior boundary is not enough to establish an
  outside day. Keep those logically different claims separate.
- **A conditional probability needs its conditional sample.** The FT71
  statistical lecture's opposite-boundary inference cannot be derived merely
  by complementing an unconditional both-boundaries percentage. See the exact
  passage in [the lecture audit](video-ft71-statistics.md).
- **Unknown is not 50/50.** Two possible outcomes and inability to predict the
  next one do not establish equal probabilities. Some FT71 risk language uses
  that simplification; retain his uncertainty lesson without adopting it.
- **A normal-looking profile is not a proved Normal distribution.** A selected
  value-area coverage setting and one-standard-deviation coverage coincide
  only under additional assumptions; the historical examples do not test them.
- **Cumulative delta is not open interest.** One VPOC teacher uses that phrase
  loosely. Aggressor imbalance is different from outstanding contracts.
- **A static profile does not identify open positions.** Narratives about
  trapped buyers/sellers or institutional defense are interpretations, not
  observed account inventories. Full Wagon is a metaphor, not a measured
  positioning indicator or proof of coordinated manipulation.
- **Motivational conviction does not create an edge.** Douglas assumes a
  qualified method; the commercial interview does not prove one. A probability
  illustration does not promise exact sample counts, independent trades or
  reliable income. The unreliable Hidden Enemy captions are not teaching evidence.
- **Historical rollover guidance differs from current practice.** Preserve the
  blog's Thursday wording as historical. The separate CME check gives its
  current customary Monday convention and allows rolling at other times;
  the core transferable requirement is contract/liquidity consistency.

## Details not recovered sufficiently for implementation

1. Exact profile anchors, binning, aggregation and value-area construction.
2. Deterministic LIS, PBT, HVN/LVN and HVE selection and their revisions.
3. Numerical acceptance/rejection criteria, time windows and persistence.
4. Rules for resolving conflicting timeframe, flow and intermarket signals.
5. An ES-specific, current, causal entry/stop/target/size policy.
6. A complete pressure-based exit rule and treatment of partial fills.
7. A reproducible statistical sample supporting Vern's listed rates or win rate.
8. Whether the static chart annotations preceded the pictured outcomes.
9. Full spoken/visual coverage beyond available captions; four sources lack
   retained usable exports and one recovered export is unreliable.

## What a future test would need

Freeze a narrow hypothesis instead of “test Vern's whole method.” For example,
define a causal prior-RTH value region, an observable re-entry condition, a
time horizon and all exclusions before measuring a proposed rotation. Separate
the descriptive path event from a feasible execution policy. Track developing
references at their availability times, actual contract prices, transaction
costs, fills, overlapping events and evaluation splits.

Those are future protocol requirements, not tests executed by this review.
The existing Abacus daily-level publications remain immutable and separate.
No source integrity check certifies the scientific or trading validity of the
claims collected here.
