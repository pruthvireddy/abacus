## What is established

The article body contains three embedded videos without an accompanying written explanation. The teaching recovered from two of them is third-party material curated by Vern: a Jigsaw presentation about related markets and an Amar/Tickstrike presentation about audio alerts. It does not establish that Vern uses every speaker's product, timeframe or management practice.

The Jigsaw speaker uses correlation as a reading of the **current local lean**: are related markets supporting a direction, opposing it, or moving independently? The relationship can change across both regimes and minutes. He rejects a permanent “NQ leads ES” rule ([10:09–11:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t5B2N2xathw&t=609s)). A thinner instrument may show a larger price move from common activity while a thicker instrument shows one-sided transactions with little displacement; equal ticks across products are not equal strength.

An entry can be delayed when related markets oppose it. A local pullback can be tolerated when the broader lean remains supportive. Conversely, a change in related-market pressure can prompt reassessment of an existing position ([16:43–19:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t5B2N2xathw&t=1003s), [29:39–31:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t5B2N2xathw&t=1779s)). His “safety net” is explicitly neither a setup nor a stop loss. His DOM-reset baseline and short chart windows belong to this scalping demonstration, not a universal Vern rule.

Amar compares index activity and large constituent stocks, treating agreement as confirmation and disagreement as a warning. His alerts express adaptive relative activity, not raw contract counts ([19:09–21:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGBZkTsoKQg&t=1149s)). A burst can accompany either continuation or exhaustion depending on the prior auction ([13:18–13:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qGBZkTsoKQg&t=798s)). Historical index weights, product claims and vendor success stories are not current verified facts or performance evidence.

## Research boundary

The Jigsaw speaker warns that violent news and the first minutes after FOMC can defeat usual comparisons; regional news can break overnight international relationships ([46:40–47:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t5B2N2xathw&t=2800s), [49:09–49:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t5B2N2xathw&t=2949s)). Neither visual resemblance nor selected vendor examples establish a stable coefficient, causal lead or executable predictive advantage. Correlated confirmations also are not independent observations. The sources provide contextual discretion, with no fixed observation window, normalized strength threshold or validated profitability rule.
