## What is established

The page supplies four curated videos and a still chart, with no substantive article prose. Their [detailed transcript review](../video-execution.md) distinguishes a working transaction reference, Jigsaw's confirmation-versus-price tradeoff, FT71's absorption demonstration and a lesson on changing activity states. These teachers illustrate principles rather than one identical execution policy attributable to Vern.

The practical thread is to start from a reasoned location, then compare the activity with its price effect. An aggressive entry can have less confirmation and a better price; waiting can reduce the remaining room. No suitable observation means pass, not necessarily reverse the premise.

FT71's replay demonstration is especially instructive because he discloses that its selected area lacks his normal preparatory context and the example loses. The knowledge base retains that failure rather than presenting perceived absorption as a reliable standalone trigger. The [actual article chart](../chart-cases.md#c02--execution-the-location-and-the-tape-answer-different-questions) separately shows profile levels and highlighted tape activity.
