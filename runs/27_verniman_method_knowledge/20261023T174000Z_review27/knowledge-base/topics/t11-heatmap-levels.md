## The source's distinction

**Source statement.** The heatmap records limit-order-book information through time. The current book is displayed to the right of the time divider; past book states appear to its left. The page explicitly says this display supplies information rather than automatic buy/sell instructions, and leaves interpretation to the trader.

**Attribution.** Much of the wording matches Bookmap's [own heatmap explanation](https://bookmap.com/blog/heatmap-in-trading-the-complete-guide-to-market-depth-visualization), including the first-person reference to educational materials. Treat this as Bookmap-derived teaching selected by Vern. The current vendor page contains later additions; those additions are not evidence of what the historical blog taught.

## How to use that distinction

**Abacus synthesis.** A visible liquidity band is a location to investigate. Its significance depends on context and subsequent interaction. Label separately the displayed orders, actual transactions, price response and the interpretation of participant intent. A static frame cannot establish the full sequence of adding, removing, trading through or replenishing orders.

No brightness threshold, persistence requirement, entry trigger or stop rule appears in this article. Its claims about accuracy and profitability remain vendor claims. A heatmap's resolution does not make motives observable or resolve whether an illustrated condition has positive expectancy.
