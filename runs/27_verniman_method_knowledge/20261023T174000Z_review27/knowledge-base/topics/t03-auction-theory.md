# T03 — Auction Theory

Source: [Auction Theory](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2013/07/using-market-delta.html). Article read. Embedded speakers are separate authors curated by Vern.

## Core teaching

The article treats the market as a complex auction whose useful organizing variable is value. Price explores the limits of buyers and sellers; volume helps distinguish acceptance from rejection. An excursion may build a new accepted area, fail toward established value, or pass through another cycle of discovery and balance. Its express ambition is explanation sufficient for decisions, rather than dependable point forecasting.

## Decision consequences

**Abacus synthesis:** ask what the current auction is testing, whether participation supports the new prices, and whether value is migrating before choosing continuation or rotation. A price crossing alone does not establish acceptance. The source gives no universal volume threshold, elapsed-time requirement or mechanical balance detector; those cannot be invented as Vern's rules.

The difficult boundary is real time: an eventual distribution can make acceptance look obvious retrospectively. A usable research specification must freeze what was visible when the decision occurred.
