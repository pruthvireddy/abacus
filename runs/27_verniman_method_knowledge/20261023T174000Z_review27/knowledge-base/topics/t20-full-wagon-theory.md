# T20 — Full Wagon Theory

Source: [Full Wagon Theory](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2014/02/the-bandwagon-theory.html). Article read. The page presents a market allegory without a visible author credit; do not assert original authorship by Vern.

## What the analogy conveys

Early participants join a moving wagon and leave while later crowds arrive. More passengers accompany less progress. An eventual reverse movement throws late entrants off; after the crowd is cleared, earlier participants return and the cycle resumes. The conceptual warning is that popularity, comfort and apparent ease can peak when continuation becomes fragile.

## Useful application and limits

**Abacus synthesis:** use the story to question a chase after substantial movement and to notice effort without progress. It can connect psychologically with absorption or exhaustion, but the article itself supplies no order-flow measurement or operational test.

The story's knowledgeable insiders and manipulated crowd are narrative devices, not observed identities. Do not infer coordination, deliberate trapping or imminent reversal from a stalled market. “Full” has no measurable denominator; the story's percentage is not a validated positioning threshold. Trend continuation can also follow a pause. A research hypothesis therefore needs observable location, pressure, response and invalidation instead of assuming the metaphor predicts the next move.
