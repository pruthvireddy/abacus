## Explicit teaching

The article connects resistance to admitting a losing trade with protecting one's self-image. It also warns that success can inflate confidence before the next decision. Its practical instructions are to reset between trades, accept the possibility of a loss, use a stop and adhere to the plan. The object of the next trade is not defending the previous result or obtaining approval.

The page mixes dramatic quotations and motivational material with trading commentary. Embedded quotations and historical attributions have not been independently authenticated; they are not needed to establish the trading lesson.

## Operational interpretation

An Abacus review can ask whether a trader kept a losing hypothesis alive because it mattered to being right, or changed size after a winner because it mattered to feeling exceptional. This converts the psychological theme into observable process questions without diagnosing the trader.

Resetting does not mean discarding market context or ignoring documented execution errors. It means that personal pride in the last outcome should not decide the next trade. That distinction is our synthesis.
