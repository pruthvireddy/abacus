# T15 — Market Excess

Source: [Market Excess](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2013/04/the-profile-traders_26.html). Article read; explanations of participant motives are source interpretations.

## Core teaching

Vern describes excess at an established new swing high as evidence that an upward auction may have finished. The distinguishing profile feature is an extended single-print tail. He offers two formation stories: an elevated opening followed by early liquidation, or an exhaustion excursion out of an existing range followed by return. Repeated small probes with no committed continuation are instead characterized as weak, short-term activity and insufficient excess.

## Application and limits

**Abacus synthesis:** separate a completed-looking rejection from repeated testing before deciding that a higher auction has ended. The text says excess can signal completion; it does not supply a reversal-entry trigger or promise a durable top.

No minimum tail length, profile period, trend qualification or invalidation rule is supplied. A screenshot cannot establish the identity or emotional state of traders. Do not confuse a thin volume tail with proven TPO single prints: the underlying constructions differ.
