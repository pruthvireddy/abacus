# T17 — Markets in Profile

Source: [Markets in Profile](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2012/01/market-profile.html). Article read. It recommends work associated with James Dalton and Peter Steidlmayer; their methods are not original inventions attributed to Vern.

## Core teaching

The article presents profile as a way to arrange price, time and volume so a trader can reason about control, accepted value and possible direction. It emphasizes judgment and practice rather than automatic buy/sell signals. Value areas represent accepted ranges; POC locates concentrated activity. Distributional anomalies are proposed as opportunities with favorable directional asymmetry and payoff potential.

## Application and unresolved construction

**Abacus synthesis:** use the profile to frame a conditional hypothesis and locate meaningful structure, then seek evidence for acting. A bell-shaped illustration does not establish normally distributed market returns, known odds or a fixed target.

The article moves between time-based profile description and trading-activity language without specifying separate TPO and volume calculations. A future test must define which profile, session, binning and value-area algorithm is intended. This distinction matters when two platforms show different POCs from the same auction.
