## The curriculum's connected model

**Curated third-party teaching.** Bruce Pringle (name as captioned), a Bookmap educator, builds the course in four layers: basic market mechanics; auction structure/profile; practical setup families; and confluence/execution. The sequence matters. An aggressive trade matters in relation to available liquidity, its price result and the larger structure. A bright heatmap line or large green dot is not enough.

Part 1 distinguishes sweeps, absorption and exhaustion. A sweep can continue or reject; it is not inherently a stop run. Absorption is aggressive flow meeting sufficient opposing liquidity without the expected further progress. Exhaustion is a decline in aggressive participation and can coexist with visible liquidity.

Part 2 distinguishes responsive activity in balance from initiative that establishes another range. It warns that profile shapes require context. Acceptance combines time, trading and the subsequent response; it remains qualitatively defined.

Part 3 gives three families: reversal after structural change at a larger level; momentum that joins initiative; and a quiet pullback after directional trading establishes a new area. Each has a different entry premise. It also discusses contrary initiative as a reason to cancel an order or exit.

Part 4 treats CVD, iceberg estimates, large-lot tracking and related markets as supplemental evidence. The presenter explicitly warns against trading these alone. Measurement windows, resets, filters and instrument liquidity matter.
