# T16 — Value Transition

Source: [Value Transition](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2013/03/trading-vpoc-shifts.html). Article read; first-person presentation is Vern's, while embedded lessons are curated external teaching.

## Core teaching

VPOC is the day's most-traded price. In the article it represents accepted value: a balanced auction may rotate around it, while a trend carries price away and subsequently builds volume at new prices. The crucial distinction is failed discovery back toward existing value versus accepted discovery that relocates value. A double distribution can retain two high-volume areas separated by a low-volume region.

Balanced conditions support fading extremes toward POC. Trending conditions support pullbacks as value follows price. A developing volume peak that becomes dominant can warn an existing directional position that the auction is settling.

## Research implications

**Abacus synthesis:** POC migration describes evolving participation, not a self-sufficient trade trigger. Freeze developing profiles; a final POC is unavailable intraday. The article gives no minimum migration distance, persistence, volume requirement, stop placement or causal day-type classifier.
