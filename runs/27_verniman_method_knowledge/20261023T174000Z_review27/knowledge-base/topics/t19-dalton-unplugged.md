# T19 — Dalton Unplugged

Source: [Dalton Unplugged](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2019/06/dalton-unplugged.html). Article read. This is Vern's selection and summary of James Dalton's interview, not evidence that every position below is Vern's own practice.

## Core teaching

Dalton organizes discretionary decisions around movement from balance to imbalance and back. Inside the prior range, patience is appropriate; an opening outside that range offers a different possibility of expansion. A break with stronger participation is more credible than one with weakening volume. Precision around obvious levels can leave a small failed auction, so levels need context rather than obsessive exactness.

Preparation should include three plausible scenarios. A single story encourages scrambling when the auction disagrees. The article names anxious morning entries, excessive frequency and overly tight stops as recurrent errors. Structural invalidation should inform risk; arbitrary dollar stops and automatic break-even moves are criticized.

## Interpretation boundary

**Abacus synthesis:** a structural stop still requires position sizing and an account-level risk limit; criticism of tight stops does not authorize unlimited loss. Neither this article nor the interview supplies a complete sizing model or universal stop distance. Its criticism of automatic break-even can coexist with other speakers' quicker management only if timeframe and strategy are kept explicit.
