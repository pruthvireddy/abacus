## Interpretation

The agenda places management inside the whole decision process: an exit cannot be assessed without knowing the planned destination and the reason for later intervention. Recordkeeping is the bridge between an explanation that feels convincing and a process that can be evaluated.

The source's explicit credit matters: selected external teaching is not proof of Vern's identical use of every demonstrated technique. The transcript reviews recover FT71's own distinctions between structural invalidation, position sizing, setup-attempt limits, daily limits, partial exits and a campaign's combined breakeven.
