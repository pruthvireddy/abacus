# T12 — Try without charts

[Blog source](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2014/01/trade-without-charts.html) · article read; two embedded videos inventoried. The [practice video note](../video-practice-and-heatmap.md) contains the reviewed lessons; the central media records give exact coverage.

## What Vern selected

**Source statement.** This article explicitly introduces Peter Davies of Jigsaw Trading. The proposed lens is the intraday volume profile: reason about where traders may be committed and how their subsequent behavior could influence entry and exit locations. The article says this lens can accompany chart-based trading; discarding all charts is not a requirement.

**Attribution.** Davies is the educator. The argument that this is a fast route to profitability for prop trainees is an instructional claim, not supporting outcome evidence. Do not relabel it as Vern's verified experience.

## The crucial inference boundary

**Abacus synthesis.** Volume traded at a price and current positions held by identifiable traders are different things. The selected teaching uses the former to hypothesize about the latter. Write such hypotheses explicitly: if traders remain committed here, what might a revisit, break or rejection make them do? Then look for the response actually observed.

This preserves the method's behavioral logic without pretending that a profile reveals every entry, exit, stop or participant identity. A level establishes possible opportunity; a response and risk boundary are still needed. The prose supplies no reproducible threshold for deciding when commitment, defense or a stop run exists.
