# T18 — Trading Value

Source: [Trading Value](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2013/03/trade-market-value.html). Article read; first-person rules are directly stated by Vern.

## Core teaching

Vern's first task is locating value, then comparing current price with it. His preferred path runs from a rejected low-volume region toward accepted high-volume trading. In balance he fades range extremes; during directional conditions he seeks pullbacks near low-volume structure as accepted value moves. LVNs are proposed entry locations and HVNs target locations. The cheap-versus-expensive analogy describes location relative to accepted value rather than an instrument's absolute price.

## Decision consequences and boundary

The qualification is essential: buying below value and selling above it persists only while the perception of value has not changed. Breaking low-volume structure can mark that change. Treating every distant price as a bargain would discard his own exception.

**Abacus synthesis:** determine which distribution supplies the reference value, mark the prospective location, and specify evidence that cancels the rotation premise. The article supplies no mechanical LVN boundary, required rejection, stop or definitive trend detector. Its pullback wording does not justify inventing a universal tick offset.
