# T08 — Using Order Flow

[Blog source](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2013/07/trade-market-value.html) · article read; three embedded videos inventoried. Exact transcript review states are recorded in the central media coverage records.

## Core claim

**Source statement.** The article frames price movement through executed orders interacting with available liquidity. It rejects the idea that an indicator or moving-average combination itself causes a move. A favorable analysis still needs other participants to generate the transaction flow that would carry price in the trade's direction.

**Attribution.** The article contains no explicit external byline in the captured body. Treat it as blog-hosted teaching, rather than proof that every sentence is Vern's original formulation. The linked videos are separate instructional sources.

## What this changes in market reading

**Abacus synthesis.** Translate a trade narrative into a behavioral proposition: who would need to buy or sell, where would that activity meet liquidity, and what observable price response would support or contradict the proposition? This makes a level an area for investigation; it does not make touching the level sufficient to trade.

Keep the execution facts separate from the story about motive. Executed volume is observable in the supplied tools. An account of forced exits, new conviction or institutional intent may be an interpretation. The article's categorical wording about price not moving without executions should not be expanded into a complete definition of quote or order-book behavior; it does not specify what it means by “price.”
