# T02 — Glossary Terms

Source: [Glossary of Terms](https://verniman.blogspot.com/2014/04/glossary-of-terms.html). Article read; Vern's definitions are historical instructional claims.

## What the vocabulary does

The glossary connects four jobs: locate value (POC, HVN, LVN, HVE), classify context (RTH/ETH, overnight and prior-day references), describe auction events (OSR, 1TF, SOC, GAP), and communicate decisions (LIS, SOH, PBT). CVD supports directional interpretation; IVR and Vern's MFE describe expected versus realized range.

Two numbers need explicit attribution: the page assigns 70% to balanced-market return to value and 80% to completing a value-area traversal after re-entry. It supplies no sample, denominator, timing qualification or estimation procedure. They remain source claims.

## Consequences and unresolved definitions

A label is a prompt to inspect context, not an order. Before implementation specify session boundaries, profile construction, reference availability and confirmation/invalidation. The page does not define the duration of IB, a mechanical OSR endpoint, value-area algorithm, PBT formula or accepted IVR units.

**Terminology collision:** Vern's MFE expands to Maximum Favorable Extension and compares range with IVR; do not silently substitute the usual trade-path excursion meaning. CVD measures classified transactions, not directly identifiable new positions.
