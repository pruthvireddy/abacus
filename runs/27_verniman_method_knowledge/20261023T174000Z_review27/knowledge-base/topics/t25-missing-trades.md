## What the post actually teaches

Vern uses Tony Robbins's golf analogy and a trading adaptation of an *Any Given Sunday* speech to emphasize small differences in timing and willingness to accept loss while pursuing an opportunity. The page is motivational. It does not define a mechanical entry, an optimal delay, a requirement to capture every tick or a rule to recover a missed move.

## How to read it with the broader method

Abacus interpretation: willingness to take a planned risk can counter hesitation, but enthusiasm alone does not identify a trade. This page needs to be read alongside the actual setup and risk rules rather than promoted into an independent trading system. A later process review could separate a qualified opportunity skipped through hesitation from a move that never met the plan.

The source's rhetorical focus on effort is not performance evidence. Nor does a narrow difference between two outcomes establish that greater effort improves a random result.
