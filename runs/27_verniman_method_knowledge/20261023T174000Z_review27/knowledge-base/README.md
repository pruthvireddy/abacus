# Verniman method knowledge base

**A source-backed reading of how Vern connects auction context, value,
location, order flow and disciplined execution.** This review covers the 27
blog topics selected by the user on 2026-10-23 and their directly embedded
YouTube material.

Start with [the connected method](method-model.md), then
[level selection and drawing limits](levels.md), the [working glossary](glossary.md)
and [actual chart readings](chart-cases.md). Use the
[reading path and practice records](practice-and-checklists.md) to study the
relationships rather than memorize isolated labels.

## How to read the evidence

- **Vern's explicit teaching:** first-person statements and definitions on his
  selected pages. Historical parameters and reported results remain attributed.
- **Curated teaching:** the lessons he links or summarizes from Dalton, Davies,
  FT71, Bookmap, Douglas and other speakers. Selection does not establish
  authorship or identical implementation.
- **Abacus synthesis:** explanatory connections, tables, checklists, critiques
  and research questions. These organize the sources without filling missing
  execution rules with invented precision.

The [source and media coverage](source-coverage.md) records what was actually
read, viewed, exported or inaccessible. The review retained 27 page captures
and 42 transcript exports from 46 distinct embedded videos. One export has
unreliable captions; four yielded no retained transcript. Six source market
charts and one instructional slide were visually inspected. Captions are not
independent audio/visual verification, and reading an entire export does not
establish complete coverage of a video's spoken content.

No market-data calculations, backtest or profitability validation were performed.
This is a critical source study. [Unknowns and disagreements](unknowns-and-tensions.md)
are part of the knowledge base, including missing level algorithms, unsupported
percentages, author differences and historical operational guidance.
