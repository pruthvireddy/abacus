## Retention and audit boundary

Original page captures, article extracts, linked image bytes and exported captions
are retained in `work/27_verniman_method_knowledge/20261023T174000Z_review27/`.
Preserve `source-captures/` and `transcripts/` as retained source evidence. Source
HTML captures have file-completion timestamps, request locators, declared canonical
URLs and SHA-256 hashes; the HTTP redirect chains/headers were not retained.
Dates inside historical URL paths do not identify the current page's last edit.

The completed run contains portable inventories, review attestations, original
notes and hashes rather than third-party full-text mirrors. Byte integrity
identifies what was retained locally; it does not authenticate trading results,
prove source authorship of reposted material or validate an empirical edge.
