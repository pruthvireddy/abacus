documents: 27 topic notes and 21 shared explanations, media studies, chart cases
and source/coverage navigation documents.

Its organizing model is Vern's larger-auction preparation, value-based location
and destination selection, observation of current order-flow response, and
disciplined execution/risk. Curated speakers retain their own methods and
decision horizons; they are not forced into a single policy.

exports (one unreliable), four unavailable exports, six inspected market charts
and one instructional slide. Full retained caption reading is not independent
audio/video-frame verification. The [coverage ledger](knowledge-base/source-coverage.md)
provides every source and exact review range.
