# Daily Map archive 2025

The 2025 Daily Map snapshot records every calendar day from January 1 through
December 31, newest first in year/month/day folders. It retains 246 dated articles
across 242 distinct dates and all 492 article image references. Content-addressed
storage preserves 489 unique image files without altering their bytes.

All article body text is preserved, including the outlook, trading plan, notes,
schedules, closing statements and outbound links. All 246 pages were freshly
fetched. Original image files already retained at the same source URLs were reused
only after verifying their exact hashes; original capture times remain recorded.
No source or image request failed, and no inline reduced-resolution fallback was
needed. This does not claim that old image bytes were newly downloaded.

## Dates and gaps

Every one of the 365 calendar dates has its own record. Nineteen weekday dates and
104 weekend dates have no observed dated post. A gap does not establish historical
absence or a holiday; no neighboring plan is substituted. The earliest stated
map date is January 2, and January 1 remains an explicit gap.

not establish historical plan availability. No undated commentary was assigned
to an in-range day in this snapshot.

Starting at the December 31, 2025 map already observed at the prior archive's
boundary, the fresh Older Post traversal crossed below January 1 to the article
stating December 31, 2024. The boundary capture is preserved but excluded from
in-range article and image counts. The official sitemap index and all three
declared sitemap pages were retained, and every one of the 246 locators between
the seed and lower boundary is represented.

The earlier reviewed 2025 collection was pinned by its manifest SHA-256 and used
only as retained source evidence. All 246 prior source/date identities remain
represented, and normalized article bodies match the earlier captures. Published
daily-level datasets and their original source files remain unchanged.

The standalone reader occupies 26,757,944 bytes (25.5 MiB). Source HTML compresses
from 12,322,532 to 2,798,735 bytes. Source capsules use gzip; original compressed
images are deduplicated by content hash without resizing or lossy recompression.
The reader uses no remote scripts, fonts or images; original outbound links remain
references. Full source bytes stay in the matching retained ignored work stage.
This completed run preserves metadata navigation, source/payload inventories,
coverage, counts, frozen code and environment, exact commands and check records.
Retain the stage as source evidence, not disposable scratch.
