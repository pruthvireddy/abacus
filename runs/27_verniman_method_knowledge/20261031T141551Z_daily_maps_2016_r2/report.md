This snapshot preserves 160 recovered outlooks across 160 dates, January 5–December
1, within a complete 366-date calendar. The surviving archive does not establish
full historical coverage. There are 101 weekday and 105 weekend gaps, no duplicate
or undated selected records, and no observed 2015 daily-map boundary. February 29
has a retained outlook. January 1 and the dates after December 1 remain explicit
gaps rather than being filled from neighboring articles.

## Historical sources and image limits

The sources are 134 archived HTML pages, one selected archived homepage post and
25 exactly selected historical Atom entries. Their title Outlook month/day and
independently reviewed 2016 context establish the recorded dates. Current URL
names, image filenames, source update claims and archive observation clocks do
not silently replace dates or establish pre-open availability.

All available source text, titles, links and 560 actual image elements are retained.
**All 560 image files are current-source fallback responses captured in 2026;
their historical byte identity is unverified.** This qualification appears beside
every image and in the reader's archive notice. Original response bytes, resolution,
source URLs and capture times remain unchanged. No historical-image identity is
inferred from filenames or apparent chart dates.

November 17, November 23 and December 1 have image-only article bodies. Their source
titles and images remain intact, with an explicit notice that there is no body text.
No text is invented or extracted numerical values substituted for the actual source.

The 135 original HTML responses remain byte exact. Each Atom entry is selected by
its exact entry ID and ordinal; its entry-only HTML view is explicitly derived.
All 25 entries link to one deduplicated, byte-exact original archived XML payload.
Source-context prose stays in retained source payloads, while run metadata retains
its hash, byte count and pinned locator. Archive observations span January 8–December
7, 2016; the retained retrieval clocks are from October 21, 2026. The offline import
is not described as a fresh download of historical pages.
