# Daily Map archive 2023

[Open the offline reader](../../../work/27_verniman_method_knowledge/20261031T043818Z_daily_maps_2023/archive/index.html) · [Markdown date index](knowledge-base/daily-map/README.md) · [Gap log](knowledge-base/daily-map/gap-log.md)

This annual snapshot records every calendar day from January 1 through December
31, 2023, newest first in year/month/day folders. It retains 248 dated articles
across 245 stated dates, complete article body text and all 496 article image
references as 496 unique files. Outlooks, plans, notes, schedules, closing text
and outbound links remain part of the retained source record.

All 248 article pages were freshly captured. Original image bytes already retained
at the same source URLs were reused only after exact hash verification; their
original capture times remain recorded. No source or image request failed and no
reduced-resolution fallback was needed. This does not claim that previously
retained image bytes were newly downloaded.

## Calendar dates and source qualifications

All 365 calendar dates have their own record. Sixteen weekdays and 104 weekend
dates have no observed dated post. A gap does not establish historical absence
or infer an exchange holiday, and no neighboring plan is substituted. The latest
stated map is December 29; December 30 and 31 remain explicit weekend gaps even
though the crawl seed precedes the requested end date. The earliest map states
January 3, leaving January 1 and 2 as explicit gaps.

Three duplicate date pairs remain separate without correcting the source text:

| Stated date | Retained articles |
|---|---|
| February 2 | Jobs Report; After FOMC |
| April 5 | Jobless Claims; ADP report |
| August 14 | China slowdown; Consumer week |

The Banks Q3 article states Outlook October 14, a Saturday, while its source
update metadata indicates October 13. The snapshot preserves the stated October
14 date. Source weekday differences, update times, image filename dates and old
permalink years do not silently replace body text. Displayed dates use Outlook
month/day and chronology anchored by the observed December 29, 2023 seed.
Capture time does not establish historical availability. No undated commentary
was assigned to an in-range date.

## Traversal and retention

The December 29 seed was already observed at the completed 2024 traversal
boundary. Fresh Older Post traversal crossed the requested January 1 lower bound
to the article stating December 30, 2022. That boundary response remains retained
but is excluded from in-range article/image counts. The official sitemap index
and all three declared sitemap pages were captured; every observed locator in
the seed-to-boundary range is accounted for.

The reviewed 2023 collection manifest was pinned by its SHA-256 and used only
as retained source evidence. Every prior source/date/body identity is represented.
Source text/image preservation is separate from the older numerical extraction
qualifications; those do not cause article exclusions. No published daily-level
dataset, original capture or completed annual run was modified.

The standalone reader occupies 17,114,153 bytes (16.3 MiB). Exact source HTML
compresses from 12,381,159 to 2,800,220 bytes. HTML/article capsules use gzip;
source images retain their original compressed bytes and available resolution,
with duplicate bytes stored once. The reader has no remote script, font or image
dependency. Outbound links remain references; video download is not claimed.

Full source bytes stay in the matching ignored work stage. This completed run
stores portable date navigation, source/payload inventories, coverage, counts,
frozen code and environment, commands and checks. Retain the source stage as
evidence, not disposable scratch.

image files, all 248 prior source/date/body identities, the complete ordered
image inventory, all sitemap locators and the December 30, 2022 boundary. All

records and all twelve months. The year-end fixture verifies December 30–31

earliest articles, loaded images, the stated Saturday map, duplicate dates,
calendar navigation and the end-of-year gaps. Prior-year preservation verifies
all 1,154 sealed artifacts, 4,965 reader files and 2,128 retained capture files
across completed 2024–2026 snapshots remain unchanged.

These checks establish source retention and presentation, not trading or
scientific validity. No level calculation, backtest, dataset release or public
