This is a complete 365-date calendar record of the currently observed sources,
not a complete recovery of historical 2017 content. Only 28 dated maps survive
in the reviewed source trail, covering November 20–December 29. Twelve additional
daily-plan bodies state only Outlook without month/day; their full text and images
remain separate and undated. No date is inferred from their November URLs,
image names, update claims or neighboring posts.

The reader preserves all 40 selected daily articles and 80 original images:
56 images belong to dated maps and 24 to undated plans. All 40 article pages were
freshly captured. Original linked image bytes were reused after exact hash
verification, with their earlier capture times retained. All 49 requests
succeeded, without image failures, mismatched assets or inline fallback.

## Gaps and the observed endpoint

All 365 dates are recorded newest first, including 232 weekday and 105 weekend
gaps. The 323 dates before November 20 have no observed dated map; no adjacent
plan or undated body fills them. December 30–31 remain explicit weekend gaps
although the source seed is December 29. No duplicate stated dates were observed.
These gaps do not prove that a post never existed.

The raw Older Post chain visits 45 pages and ends at Trading Process, vtp.html,
which has no Older Post link. No 2016 daily-plan boundary was observed. The
fresh official sitemap index and all three pages account for the inclusive
45-locator seed-to-terminal range. The pinned completion review also documents
previous public-feed exhaustion at 2,207 entries. Neither observation establishes
historical completeness. Current source publication claims of 2000 and later
update years remain metadata, rather than map dates or historical availability.

## Educational context remains separate

Five reviewed educational/platform pages, with 15 images, remain retained raw in
capture-manifest.json's excluded_context_posts and the matching work stage.
They include Trading Tips, TOS screen, TOS parameters, Balance Target and Trading
Process. Some images show later material. They are outside the 40 daily-plan
articles and are not treated as dated or undated 2017 maps.

The collector accepts this separation only with matching requested scope, verified
completion/chronology review bytes, original HTML identities and normalized body
hashes. A changed or dated body is refused. The observed terminal stops fallback
into unrelated sitemap content, while the inclusive sitemap range remains checked.
The twelve undated daily plans remain in the selected posts array.
