# VWAP statistical execution protocol 1.0.0

Frozen before inspecting strategy outcome tables on 2026-09-22. The original six-candidate [plan](plan.md) remains historical context. This execution covers only `vwap_return_after_departure` (B), `vwap_extreme_fade` (A), and `vwap_first_retest_bounce` (C), in that order. This is price-path research, not a net trading backtest.

## Population and time

Use the exact pinned `session_anchored_vwap__1.0.0` 2m and 5m datasets and `volume_lead_unadjusted_v1` 1m source. Include every eligible ES, NQ, and GC product-session through 2026-09-18. Split by session date: development through 2023, validation 2024, final 2025-01-01 onward. The final period is chronological evaluation, not certified unseen data. Days are retrospectively complete; this is sample selection, never an entry-time feature.

Create seven correlated anchor/window views per product, day, and timeframe: Globex/Globex, Asia/Asia, London/London, NY/NY, and Globex in each of Asia, London, NY. Local windows use their published membership; Globex is 18:00 preceding date to 17:00. A bar belongs to a view only if its entire interval is in the window. Close at the exclusive window end is allowed as a signal but has zero follow-up. Keep anchor and window IDs distinct. Never sum views as independent opportunities.

An anchor is eligible at signal availability if active, VWAP and sigma are finite, sigma > 0, and `available_at - anchor_ts >= 30 minutes`. Warm-up sensitivities use 20 and 40 elapsed minutes. Reconcile each feature bar with exactly 2 or 5 consecutive 1m bars, identical product/day/contract identity, unadjusted OHLC plus the feature adjustment, and a constant adjustment within the episode. Fail the run on a mismatch. Verify SHA-256 for the lock files and every consumed year partition.

The first 1m bar at or after `available_at` supplies the movement reference open. It must share the session and contract. For fixed horizons, require the complete 10/30/60-minute interval before the observation-window end; mark late events explicitly. A missing reference or source gap makes the horizon unevaluable. No path bridges a roll, session, or window. A completed 1m bar at the horizon boundary is included; signed endpoint movement uses its close. Fixed-horizon and window-end populations are separate.

All levels from feature bars are translated to unadjusted price by subtracting `price_adjustment * 1e-9`; price OHLC and tick size are scaled by `1e-9`. Range overlap with the inclusive VWAP ±1 tick zone is contact. A gap from wholly one side to wholly the other with no range overlap is a separate gap-through. Frozen VWAP is the level at departure/signal. For evolving VWAP, a tested feature bar uses the latest same-anchor level with `available_at <= bar_open`, fixed across its 1m sub-bars. Return clocks are independent. VWAP motion and actual price retracement are reported separately.

For movement barriers, distance is `ceil(factor * signal_sigma / tick) * tick`, with primary factor 0.5 and one-at-a-time sensitivities 0.25 and 1.0. A and C direction is +1 for long, -1 for short. At each 1m open, a gap past one barrier determines first passage; if both extremes cross opposing barriers within one 1m bar, order is ambiguous. Otherwise retain favorable first, adverse first, neither, and ambiguous, all in the denominator. The 4/8/16/32-tick symmetric grids are exploratory. Full-horizon MFE/MAE and pre-first-barrier excursions are separate. Levels already passed at the reference are flagged and get no fresh success credit.

## B: return after meaningful departure

Qualifying close has `abs(close - VWAP) >= max(sigma, 4 ticks)` with equality accepted. Primary unit is the first such completed close per view. Classify it as already away if it is the first mature valid close in that view, else fresh. Preserve no-departure windows. A frozen return uses the signal VWAP ±1 tick; evolving return follows the causal rule above. Each episode ends on frozen contact for frozen-cycle counts; a new meaningful departure after that can start another episode. Evolving cycles are separately labeled. Non-return by window end is failure for the deadline rate and administrative censoring for time-to-event summaries. A zero-follow-up departure stays in the frequency denominator and is excluded from conditional return estimation.

## A: first close outside 2σ

Use only a published `exit_above_upper_2sigma` or `exit_below_lower_2sigma` equal to true, not null, after a valid consecutive comparison within the view. A prior outside state at first eligible observation is an already-outside episode, not a fresh signal. A signal points toward VWAP. One signal per excursion. Rearm after a completed close within inclusive ±1σ; reset at window end. The primary endpoint is favorable 0.5σ before equal adverse by 30 minutes. The prespecified lesser-departure comparison uses first fresh crosses beyond ±1σ while no ±2σ exit has occurred in the episode; the control is fixed at its own signal even if price later reaches 2σ. Compare common 0.5σ movement barriers and disclose disjoint starting-distance support.

## C: first causal VWAP retest

Following a qualifying B-style departure, retain the departure's side. The first later causal VWAP-zone contact consumes it. A qualifying retest requires completed same-side closes at touch-open minus 10 minutes through touch-open inclusive: six consecutive 2m closes or three consecutive 5m closes, all valid in the same view. Equality or an opposite close resets the strict-side history. An unqualified first contact consumes the departure. A gap-through is recorded separately and resets the episode. A fresh meaningful departure may rearm. The touching bar's close determines confirmation (strictly beyond the known zone on the approach side), inside/neutral, or beyond the opposite boundary; equality is neutral. Signal availability is the touching bar close; no touch-bar extreme enters the post-signal outcome. The primary all-touch endpoint uses the same symmetric 0.5σ/30-minute barrier as A. Confirmation is a separate subset. A near-approach control is a bar whose range comes within 2 ticks but stays strictly outside the 1-tick zone at that bar's open-known VWAP; classification uses only that completed bar, and later contact does not relabel it.

## Comparisons and uncertainty

Primary comparison family: A versus its lesser-departure control and C versus later qualifying retests and near approaches, within product × timeframe × anchor/window. B is descriptive; its primary estimate is frozen return by window end. Matching strata are product, timeframe, anchor/window, direction, hour-of-window (2-hour bins), anchor age (30–60, 60–120, 120+ minutes), and observable sigma in development-tertile bins. Learn sigma cutoffs from development only, apply unchanged in validation/final, and report unmatched counts. Distance is reported, not matched for A because >2σ and 1–2σ supports differ. Within a stratum, weight controls to treated counts. Primary intervals resample whole session dates with shared views/timeframes/products held together; week-block resampling is sensitivity. Use 95% percentile intervals and mark degenerate all/none strata. Formal baseline claims require simultaneous max-statistic bootstrap intervals across the declared family; if implementation cannot support this, label comparisons exploratory without significance claims.

Report event-weighted and session-weighted summaries; all segmentation and threshold grids are exploratory. Inspect deterministic examples chosen by earliest timestamp within product/outcome class. Do not tune after validation without a decision-log entry; freeze before final evaluation. The 2m and 5m views and overlapping anchors are correlated.
