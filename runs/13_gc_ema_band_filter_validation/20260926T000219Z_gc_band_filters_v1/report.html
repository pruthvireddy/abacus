Can a close inside the candle’s upper half improve this GC long setup?

The exact rule and comparison

base signal: completed close ≤ EMA225 − 3 × residual sigma
filter: (close − low) / (high − low) ≥ 0.5
entry: next complete 5-minute bar’s unadjusted open

The high, low, and close of the signal candle are all known when it closes. The condition requires a positive candle range. Filtered-out candidates do not start the 10-bar cooldown. The primary measurement uses 20 subsequent five-minute bars and excludes incomplete, gapped, or cross-contract paths.

The score is the mean of capped MFE/σ minus capped MAE/σ, with each side capped at 10σ. “Δ vs base” compares this rule with unfiltered lower-band signals in the same year. MAE and MFE are separate extrema, so the score is a path descriptor, not profit.

What improved, and what did not

2025 selection

Among nine frozen filters, the upper-half close had the best qualifying 2025 score. Mean MAE fell from 11.77 to 10.04 GC points; mean MFE moved from 11.50 to 11.24. The filtered group had 148 valid paths versus 207 unfiltered.

2026 chronological check

Mean MAE moved from 30.94 to 30.31 points, while mean MFE moved from 23.91 to 25.00. The normalized score improved from -0.309σ to -0.179σ, but remained negative. Its week-bootstrap difference interval [-0.05, +0.32] crosses zero.

This is a directional improvement in the prespecified descriptive score. The interval is broad, and the 2026 results from the earlier unfiltered study had already been viewed. The filter is a candidate for further testing, not an execution recommendation.

Method and limits

  • Only 2025 and 2026 YTD signal outcomes were analyzed. The published EMA and sigma retain older chart history needed for warm-up. The 2026 source ends September 18.
  • Nine simple filters and fixed thresholds were declared after a feature-only support check. The 2025 selection is still exploratory. The 2026 check is chronological but not untouched because its unfiltered results were already known.
  • Confidence intervals resample ISO weeks jointly for each filter and unfiltered cohort. They are approximate, ignore the nine-candidate search, and should not be read as a calibrated probability of edge.
  • Filtered signals are re-cooled before outcomes are summarized. Excluded paths remain unavailable rather than zero. The underlying continuous chart is a final backward-adjusted snapshot and may differ from TradingView.
  • There is no stop, target, fill sequence, transaction cost, spread, slippage, position overlap rule, or realized P&L. Five-minute OHLC cannot establish whether an adverse or favorable extreme occurred first.
Source: pinned GC five-minute and EMA-band releases plus the sealed study 12 run. This report renders only saved study 13 tables. Full candidate evidence and provenance accompany the run.
