# NQ advanced AMD source study

Protocol 1.0.0 · qualitative source review · 2026-10-05.

## Question and scope

What does Tmac's linked advanced AMD lesson actually teach, how do its chart
examples support its decision process, and which claims would need independent
testing before becoming an Abacus strategy?

Review the entire video, including the introduction before the supplied 16-second
start offset. Read the exported English automatic captions and visually inspect
selected charts. Save representative chart screenshots, timestamped observations,
a claim ledger, critical interpretation, and a reproducible evidence inventory.
No market-data backtest, strategy implementation, public publication, or trade
execution is part of this review.

## Source and observation units

Primary source: [Tmac's advanced AMD lesson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mmem9CI7vvw).
Observation units are the lesson's timestamped explanations and selected chart
examples, not independent trades. The five discussed trading days belong to one
selected week and must not be treated as five independent validation samples.

The source and initial transcript were inspected before this protocol was written.
This is an exploratory source review, not a preregistered empirical experiment.
Automatic captions may mistranscribe specialist terms. Preserve uncertainty rather
than silently expand unclear acronyms or infer unshown rules.

## Method

1. Record source identity, duration, access date, publication date as displayed by
   YouTube, caption provenance, and the chart-period evidence.
2. Review all caption segments and capture the weekly context, Monday sweep,
   quarterly argument, Tuesday entries, Wednesday order-block chain and weaker
   alternative, Thursday rejection, and Friday news/entry/target decision.
3. Record the actual paused player time for every screenshot. Images are source
   observations, not newly generated market data. Preserve chart annotations as
   displayed; do not digitize approximate prices into executable trade levels.
4. Separate presenter claims, visible chart observations, reviewer inferences, and
   future test proposals. Mark reported point/R figures as unaudited examples.
5. Identify causal timing, invalidation, selection effects, undefined parameters,
   and the distinction between conditional bias and entry confirmation.
6. Stage under work, verify links/images/source hashes, freeze settings and
   provenance, then seal a unique qualitative review bundle under runs.

## Exclusions and ambiguity

No endorsement of mentorship or promotional claims. No inferred fills, returns,
win rate, execution costs, or universal quarterly-candle property. Embedded clips
cannot independently authenticate when the original private messages were sent.
Missing definitions remain missing. A later proposed study requires its own
frozen quantitative protocol and pinned input releases.

## Verification

Visually inspect every retained screenshot; match its paused timestamp and caption
context; verify local links and SHA-256 artifact/source identities; record review
coverage and all unresolved terminology. Run the repository documentation checker
and regenerate/check the research index after registration. Do not run numerical
suites or rebuild Narada for this source-only review.
