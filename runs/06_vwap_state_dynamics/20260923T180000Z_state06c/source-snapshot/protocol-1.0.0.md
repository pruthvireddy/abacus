# VWAP state dynamics executable protocol 1.0.0

Frozen 2026-09-23 18:09 UTC after the feature-only pilot `20260923T180000Z_state06c` and its source-key audit, before any new future-outcome summaries were inspected. The machine-readable companion [protocol-1.0.0.json](protocol-1.0.0.json) is the exact executed settings file. The initial two abandoned feature pilots (`state06`, `state06b`) exposed, respectively, local-anchor indexing and horizon-coverage defects; neither generated future outcomes. The corrected pilot checked all input and partition hashes, all development feature distributions, and 30-minute source keys.

## Population and causal timing

Primary cohort: one Globex-anchor, 2-minute landmark per eligible product-session at each NY local minute 00 or 30. Require a full 30-minute anchor maturity before the 30-minute exact lookback and 30 minutes remaining in the Globex window. Globex can first qualify at 19:00 NY local. Last 15 completed closes versus their own then-available VWAP define the residence state; the close at `available_at` is observable at that exclusive bar close. The preceding exact 30-minute close and VWAP changes use the completed bar ending at `t-30m`. The current day's retrospective release eligibility is a sample condition, never a live feature.

Keep 2-minute Asia, London, and NY local anchors in their published membership windows, and 5-minute Globex, as **separate** replications. Local NY first qualifies at 10:30. The 5-minute state is a 6-close analogue with the thresholds in JSON and is not pooled with the 2-minute primary cohort. Future bars remain inside each view's own window. A missing source minute, roll, changed adjustment, reference, anchor, or contract makes an outcome unavailable; it is never false. Every feature bar and future completed close is reconciled against pinned unadjusted one-minute bars. Adjusted features and references translate to contract price by subtracting the session's fixed `price_adjustment × 1e-9`.

## Fixed feature classes and comparisons

Q1 retains the original illustrative 2-minute cutoffs unchanged: persistent means 12/15 closes on the current nonzero side and at most one adjacent nonzero side change; rotational means 6–9 of 15 closes above and at least three such changes; all other valid landmarks remain `other`. Equality is neutral and counted. `Y1` is no opposite completed close against the evolving causal VWAP at or before 30 minutes. Report first-cross minute and frozen-landmark-VWAP residence sensitivity. Compare persistent to rotational, raw and on common support, with side and context denominators.

Q2 labels a slope nonflat when absolute 30-minute adjusted close movement is at least **0.016** prior ATR14 and VWAP movement at least **0.0023** prior ATR14. Same signs are aligned; opposite signs are opposing; all others are flat or unavailable. These are rounded development-period lower-quartile magnitudes (observed 0.016007 and 0.002333); each product has thousands of opposing examples. `Y2` is the complement of `Y1` for fully observed nonzero-side landmarks. Compare opposing to aligned, raw and on common support, adding Q1 state to context.

The common secondary price path for Q1/Q2 starts at the following unadjusted one-minute open. Its 30-minute four-way first-passage labels use (a) fixed 8 ticks and (b) `ceil(0.5 × landmark sigma / tick)` ticks. The current side is favorable. An opening gap is ordered before bar extremes; two barriers reached only within the same minute are ambiguous. Show all four classes and full denominators, by product and condition. These definitions cannot be selected after outcomes.

Q3 uses the cumulative equal-weighted HLC3 of the *same completed output bars and anchor* as VWAP. Both frozen references are compared to the median of the next 30 unadjusted one-minute closes. Strictly smaller absolute error is a VWAP win; error equality within `1e-8` is a tie, retained in the full denominator. TWAP wins and last-known-price absolute error are paired baselines. Report paired mean/median `(VWAP error - TWAP error)`, and the prespecified materially separated subset: absolute VWAP–TWAP distance at least **2 ES**, **6 NQ**, or **3 GC** ticks. Those integer cutoffs are the ceiling of the product's development lower-quartile separation (1.507, 5.796, 2.428 ticks). Near-identical and tied cases remain visible. Future volume-weighted price and horizon-end close are secondary targets, never feature inputs.

## Context and estimation

Prior ATR14 uses the 14 most recent prior eligible complete product sessions; the current day is excluded. Product-specific development ATR terciles are fixed in the JSON. Matching strata use product, current side, absolute distance to VWAP in sigma bins `<0.5`, `[0.5,1)`, `1+`, four named local clock windows, ATR tercile, and anchor age `<240` or `240+` minutes. Q2 also matches on residence state. Within a common stratum, control events receive the treated count as weight. Report raw and common-support differences, retained and unmatched counts, and no extrapolation. If support is weak, say so. The clock windows cover local membership gaps: evening/Asia `18:00–02:59`, London-to-NY `03:00–09:29`, NY morning `09:30–11:59`, NY afternoon `12:00–17:00`.

Report landmark- and product-session-weighted rates, product, current side, clock, age, year, and chronological split. Development ends 2023, 2024 is labeled validation, and 2025–2026 final; **all currently available dates are exploratory** due to previously viewed VWAP outcomes. Resample whole shared calendar dates, preserving correlated products and views, with 400 deterministic percentile-bootstrap repetitions. No formal three-question familywise superiority claim is attempted. An observed path probability is not a trade win rate or proof of net profitability; fills, costs, latency, sizing, and calibration models are out of scope.

The primary report schema is stable-ID CSV tables (`funnel`, `primary_probabilities`, `matched_comparisons`, `barrier_outcomes`, `paired_errors`, `breakdowns`, `replications`, `examples`, `exclusions`), `metrics.json`, per-partition event Parquet, figures, and offline HTML/Markdown derived from those tables. Secondary volume-shock, handoff, prior-session, rolling-VWAP, and range-position ideas remain a separate **unexecuted exploratory family**; a frozen amendment or new run is required before their outcomes are computed.
