6E NEWS REACTION STUDY
Completed October 17, 2026

Period: October 17, 2025 through October 16, 2026, New York dates.
Instrument: CME:6E1! continuous, back-adjusted Euro FX futures.
Data: 23,685 native TradingView 15-minute bars. Historical TradingView
economic calendar releases were checked against official BLS/BEA/Fed/ECB
schedules where material. The original Forex News indicator uses Forex
Factory metadata; its preset DXY ranks were not used as research results.

MAIN FINDING
Payrolls, Fed policy decisions and post-meeting conferences, and U.S. CPI
formed the strongest recurring scheduled-news group in this sample.
Payrolls led the first 30 minutes. The full hour following the Fed decision,
including its conference, had the larger median movement.

OTHER RELEVANT FINDINGS
ECB decisions: median 39.5 ticks / 19.75 pips over 30 minutes, eight dates.
Seven have nearby core releases, leaving only one isolated observation.
ECB conferences: median 23.5 ticks / 11.75 pips, eight dates; six have nearby
core releases. These are descriptive event-window results; a standalone ECB
ranking is not supported by this sample.

PCE and GDP: nine of 11 PCE release timestamps coincide with GDP. Keep their
results as combined release packages rather than attributing the move to
either report separately. Quarterly GDP subcategories also have small counts.

Minutes differ from decisions: FOMC minutes had an isolated 30-minute median
of 18.5 ticks / 9.25 pips across eight observations. The supplied indicator
groups titles containing FOMC together; its displayed rank remains a generic
DXY preset, not a dynamically computed 6E rank.

Large observed examples (associated movements, not causal attribution):

2026-10-14 08:30 NY: CPI package, 142 ticks / 71.0 pips.
2026-02-11 08:30 NY: employment report, 137 ticks / 68.5 pips.
2025-10-29 14:00 NY: FOMC decision, 115 ticks / 57.5 pips, before conference.

AUDIT AND LIMITS
The 24 overlapping export bars agree after normalization to 6E ticks.
Independent calculations reproduced all 1,605 valid measurement rows and
521 ranking rows. Strict contiguous-bar checks reject windows with missing
pre-release or post-release prices, including the November 2025 outage.
535 release timestamps have valid 15/30/60-minute outcomes; 362 non-speech
timestamps meet the primary 30-minute isolation rule.

One stale GDP row originally dated October 30, 2025 was excluded after BEA
confirmed the shutdown postponed that initial release to December 23.
CPI for the October 2025 reference month was canceled; September CPI was
released October 24. Historical PMI actual values are unavailable for
licensing reasons, but their timestamps remain usable for price responses.
The calendar is not a guaranteed as-of vintage.

This is one year and an exploratory comparison across many categories.
Samples, particularly central-bank and quarterly data, are small; bootstrap
intervals overlap. The numerical ordering is not a stable permanent ranking.
Fifteen-minute bars cannot measure first-second reactions, spread or slippage.
Unscheduled headlines, geopolitics, positioning and other confounders remain.
No causal-impact, forecast-surprise or directional profitability claim is made.
The ten-clean-control minimum could not be met for payrolls/CPI; no uplift
ratio against quiet periods is reported for those categories.
