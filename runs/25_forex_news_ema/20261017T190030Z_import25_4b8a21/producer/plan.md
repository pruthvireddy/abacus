# Import and present the final Forex News analysis

## Authorized scope

Bring the final saved Forex News / News EMA Move analysis from `tv-indicators`
into Abacus, prepare an accessible report, and hand the selected publication to
Narada for the user's requested Vercel release. Preserve the original source
files and wording. This protocol authorizes saved-evidence import and presentation;
it does not authorize a fresh numerical study or regenerate historical outputs.

## Source selection and protocol

The authoritative final analysis is `cross_currency_news_study/extended_year`:
`report.txt`, `summary.json`, `validation.json`, `reconstruction_audit.json`,
`overall.csv`, `by_category.csv`, `monthly.csv`, `events.csv`, and `horizons.csv`.
Retain the earlier `native57` tables and `news_ema_move_checks` as explicitly
labelled context, alongside both Pine indicators and the source analysis code.
Inventory large original price histories with exact locators, sizes and hashes.
The original project remains untouched and no source script is executed.

The final sample uses six futures (6E, 6B, 6J, 6S, 6C, 6A), the same 64 U.S.
release timestamps, and H5/H10/H15/H50/H100. The entry bar ends at the release;
its EMA20 versus EMA50 fixes direction and its close is an analytical benchmark.
The source code's `bars[i+1:i+1+h]` starts measurement at the bar opening at the
release. One observation per simultaneous release timestamp; overlapping event
windows remain separate observations. Monthly ownership is America/Chicago.

## Presentation and verification

Copy saved common-event rows and selected columns only; do not recompute averages,
rates, ratios or uncertainty. Convert source percentage points to ratios only for
Narada's percent formatter. Keep original CSV strings unchanged in four selected
summary downloads. Draw saved signed-close percentage values by horizon and saved
H5 MFE/MAE ticks. Record every numeric/table/download/figure input cell with its
source row and source column; keep exact source and output hashes.

Before sealing, verify byte-identical copies, source validation hash references,
source-cell transfers, asset budgets and Narada's dry-run source contract. Record
the exact commands, current Git revision and dirty snapshot, source producer
bytes and environment locks. Seal once into a fresh run. Integrity and presentation
checks do not repeat the original numerical validation or establish profitability.

## Interpretation boundaries

Keep negative 6A results and weak or horizon-sensitive 6J outcomes visible.
Show that longer horizons need not improve directional closes and preserve the
negative flash-PMI category results. Small category/month denominators, shared
macro shocks and overlapping FOMC windows prevent independent-trial inference.
The final year is an analytical OHLC/EMA reconstruction, rather than a direct
full-year native indicator export; native subset agreement does not remove this
distinction. Corrected calendars are retrospective, prices are back-adjusted,
and benchmark closes are not guaranteed executable fills. No costs, stops,
targets or portfolio sizing were modeled. No confidence intervals or holdout
tests are supplied in the selected source evidence.
