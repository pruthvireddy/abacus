Signal and entry

The pasted Pine indicator defines a 225-close EMA and the population standard deviation of the most recent 225 signed close minus EMA residuals. The lower outer band is three sigmas below that EMA. We use the bar’s completed close at or below this band; the script’s separate inner-band re-entry marker is a different rule.

signal: close[t] ≤ EMA225[t] − 3 × STDEV225(close − EMA225)[t]
entry: next complete 5-minute bar's unadjusted open

The band includes the signal close and becomes available at the bar close. There is no intrabar entry at the band.

What the excursions measure

MAE(H) = max(0, entry − min(low[t+1 … t+H]))
MFE(H) = max(0, max(high[t+1 … t+H]) − entry)

MAE and MFE are separate extremes. A five-minute OHLC bar does not reveal whether its high or low happened first, so these values cannot be combined into a realizable target-and-stop trade. “Terminal +” is only the fraction whose last close exceeds the entry; it is not a win rate after costs.

Reading the evidence

  • Signals after cooldown are based on accepted completed-bar signals. Suppressed candidates do not restart the cooldown. The next eligible signal is C+1 bars after an accepted signal.
  • Valid paths need complete five-minute bars with continuous timestamps, one raw contract, and one adjustment from signal through the entire horizon. Gaps, partial bars, and cross-contract paths are excluded.
  • Year is the signal close’s New York calendar year. 2010 starts in June; 2026 ends with the pinned September 18 source data. Yearly sample sizes differ.
  • The signal uses a final backward-adjusted continuous series. Its earlier chart values can incorporate later roll adjustments and are not point-in-time certified; the adjusted chart may differ from TradingView. Excursions use reconstructed unadjusted selected-contract OHLC.
  • Overlapping signals and 35 displayed combinations create dependence and selection risk. Approximate week-clustered mean intervals are in the saved table, with no multiple-comparison correction. No fill, spread, slippage, fees, sizing, stop, or exit policy is modeled.
